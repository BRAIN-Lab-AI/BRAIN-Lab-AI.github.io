Welcome to the BRAIN Lab

Welcome to the BRAIN Lab: Breakthrough Research in Next Gen AI Networks Lab! We are a research lab focused on advancing the fields of artificial intelligence, classical machine learning, deep learning, and computer vision. Our lab explores innovative methods to enable machines to perceive, understand, and interpret visual information from the world around them. Our works have applications in healthcare, autonomous systems, and human-computer interaction.

Founder and Director: Dr. Muzammil Behzad

Research Areas

Anything AI:

Exploring all aspects of Intelligent systems, reasoning, optimization, trustworthy AI.

Representation Learning:

Self-supervised, contrastive, metric, and multimodal representation learning.

2D/3D Vision:

Object detection, segmentation, scene understanding, 3D vision, and video analytics.

Multimodal AI:

Learning and reasoning across vision, language, audio, sensor, and biomedical data.

LLMs & VLMs:

Foundation models for language, vision, multimodal reasoning, retrieval, and generative AI.

Generative AI:

Diffusion models, GANs, large generative models, and synthetic data generation.

Applications of Computer Vision:

Healthcare, robotics, autonomous systems, smart cities, industrial inspection, and intelligent surveillance.

Agentic AI:

Autonomous AI agents, planning, reasoning, tool use, and multi-agent collaboration.

Affective Computing:

Facial expression analysis, emotion recognition, body gesture understanding, and behavioral AI.

Human-Computer Interaction:

Human-AI collaboration, intelligent interfaces, explainable AI, and user-centered systems.

AI for Healthcare:

Medical image analysis, clinical decision support, biomedical AI, and healthcare automation.

Explainable & Trustworthy AI:

Interpretable models, fairness, robustness, uncertainty estimation, and responsible AI.
