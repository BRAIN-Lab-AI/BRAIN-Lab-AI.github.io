University / Institute Name

Intelligent Sensing & Vision Lab

We develop machine learning and signal processing methods for healthcare, computer vision, and human-centered AI. Our current focus includes microwave imaging for stroke detection, 3D/4D facial expression analysis, and multimodal vision-language models.

About

What we do and why it matters

Our group advances foundational and applied research at the intersection of sensing, machine learning, and human health. We collaborate with clinical and industry partners to translate algorithms into real-world impact, with open-source tools and reproducible science as core principles.

Research

Current projects and directions

Microwave Imaging for Stroke

Signal processing • Tomographic reconstruction

Fast brain imaging from multi-antenna S-parameters (S11, S21…) using physics-informed learning for early stroke detection.
